# Code of conduct

We want this project to be a welcoming place for everyone, whatever their background or experience.

Be kind and assume good intent. Disagree about ideas, not people. Harassment, personal attacks and discrimination aren't accepted, in any project space.

Maintainers may remove comments or contributions that break this code, and may ban people who repeatedly or seriously break it.

To report a problem, write to hello@nodrail.com. Reports are handled in confidence.
